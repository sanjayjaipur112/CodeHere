Features

WASender is a powerful WhatsApp automation and lead-generation desktop application designed for businesses, agencies, and marketing teams that need to scale customer outreach efficiently. The platform combines advanced messaging automation, data extraction, lead management, scheduling, and AI-powered sales workflows into a single WinForms solution.

Core Messaging and Automation
- Send messages to single contacts, groups, and bulk recipients
- Schedule campaigns for future delivery
- Automate repetitive sales and support conversations
- Configure auto-reply workflows for common customer interactions
- Support multiple message templates, captions, and marketing flows
- Run scheduled and recurring outreach operations

Lead Generation and Contact Management
- Extract contacts from WhatsApp groups and discussions
- Capture active members and group metadata
- Import and manage contact lists from CSV and related formats
- Filter leads based on numbers, labels, and custom rules
- Organize prospects and leads in structured internal records
- Track lead sources, statuses, and engagement activity

Group and Network Tools
- Manage WhatsApp groups efficiently
- Add members to groups
- Detect active members
- Retrieve group links from web sources
- Generate and validate contact lists for campaign use
- Launch and monitor team/group-based messaging workflows

Web Extraction and Data Mining
- Scrape website and business information
- Extract emails, contacts, and structured company data
- Mine social media and web-based lead sources
- Build custom data collection workflows from online sources
- Support research-driven outreach and prospect enrichment

Marketing and Outreach Optimization
- Launch warm-up and lead nurturing campaigns
- Qualify leads with structured engagement paths
- Use message buttons, templates, and rules to drive conversations
- Manage multiple campaign flows from a single dashboard
- Build reusable outreach steps for sales and follow-up sequences

AI-Powered Marketing Intelligence
- Integrate Claude AI for intelligent sales conversations
- Qualify leads automatically using natural-language interaction
- Detect customer intent across product inquiries, orders, and appointments
- Recommend next-best actions for sales and support teams
- Build and test conversational bot flows for 24/7 engagement
- Support automated qualification, follow-up, and escalation logic

Sales Workflow Automation
- Track leads in an organized lead board
- Monitor order status and sales pipeline activity
- Capture appointment requests and scheduled meetings
- View campaign and bot performance through a central dashboard
- Manage orders, bookings, and outreach in one unified workflow

Appointment and Order Management
- Record and view customer appointment requests
- Track appointment status, timing, and channels
- Capture sales orders and product conversations
- Monitor pending, confirmed, and completed transactions
- Maintain a structured record of conversions and follow-ups

Analytics and Dashboarding
- View lead count, qualification trends, and engagement patterns
- Track order activity and appointment volume
- Monitor campaign health and AI bot performance
- Access a summary dashboard for growth-related KPIs
- Support better decision-making for sales outreach and automation

Bot Builder and Campaign Flow Management
- Create custom bot flows for marketing journeys
- Define staged conversations for:
  - lead qualification
  - order intake
  - appointment booking
  - reminders and follow-up
- Test flows inside the application before deployment
- Extend flows for human handoff when needed

Local Data and System Integration
- Use SQLite for persistent local campaign, lead, and order storage
- Keep marketing operations organized and easy to extend
- Support future integrations with CRM, ERP, and messaging systems
- Maintain reusable data models for automation workflows

Security and Reliability
- Support secure configuration for API-based AI integrations
- Maintain local storage for operational data
- Provide modular architecture for future expansion and upgrades
- Support business-grade automation workflows with stable WinForms deployment

In summary, WASender combines traditional WhatsApp automation with next-generation AI marketing operations, enabling teams to generate leads, qualify prospects, capture orders, schedule appointments, and scale customer engagement through conversational workflows powered by Claude AI.
